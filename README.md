# Awesome-Construction-ERP

## Top Construction ERP Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Job Costing, Project Accounting, AIA Billing, Payroll, Equipment & Construction Financials*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Construction ERP**. These systems integrate financials, job costing, project accounting, billing, payroll, and operations specifically for general contractors, specialty trades, and construction firms.



**Examples** include Viewpoint Vista, CMiC, Trimble Viewpoint, Sage Intacct Construction, Foundation Software, Acumatica Construction Edition, ComputerEase, Jonas Construction, Deltek ComputerEase, and Premier Construction Software (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: True construction ERP (job cost, WIP, certified payroll, AIA billing) is largely commercial. Solid open foundations exist via **ERPNext**, **BuildSuite**, and **Odoo** construction-oriented modules. This section expands those options while remaining realistic about feature depth.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Viewpoint Vista](https://www.viewpoint.com/)**  

  Enterprise construction ERP (Trimble) connecting financials, job costing, project operations, and payroll for larger contractors.



- **[CMiC](https://cmicglobal.com/)**  

  Full construction ERP covering project management, financials, and field operations for mid-to-large contractors.



- **[Trimble Viewpoint](https://www.viewpoint.com/)**  

  Suite of construction ERP and related products (Vista, Spectrum, and others) under the Trimble umbrella.



- **[Sage Intacct Construction](https://www.sage.com/)**  

  Cloud financial ERP with strong construction job costing and project accounting, often paired with field tools.



- **[Foundation Software](https://www.foundationsoft.com/)**  

  Construction-focused accounting and ERP popular with mid-market contractors for job cost and financials.



- **[Acumatica Construction Edition](https://www.acumatica.com/)**  

  Cloud-native ERP tailored for general contractors and specialty trades with robust job costing and project financials.



- **[ComputerEase / Deltek ComputerEase](https://www.deltek.com/)**  

  Construction accounting and ERP software widely used for job costing, billing, and project financial management.



- **[Jonas Construction](https://www.jonasconstruction.com/)**  

  Construction ERP and management software for contractors seeking integrated financial and project control.



- **[Premier Construction Software](https://www.premierconstructionsoftware.com/)**  

  Construction ERP and project management platform for contractors and builders.



- **[Related construction financial suites](https://www.sage.com/)**  

  Broader category includes Sage 100/300 CRE and other construction accounting systems still widely deployed.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[ERPNext (Construction modules)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open-source ERP with construction-oriented capabilities—projects, subcontracting, BOQ, materials, and job-style costing on the Frappe framework.



- **[BuildSuite Core](https://github.com/BuildSuite-io/buildsuite_core)**  

  Open-source construction operating system built on Frappe/ERPNext—projects, work packages, stages, site governance, and construction domain models (MIT).



- **[Odoo Community + construction apps](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Modular open-source ERP; community and third-party apps extend Project, Inventory, Accounting, and Field Service for construction use cases.



- **[OpenProject](https://github.com/opf/openproject)**  

  Open-source project management platform often used alongside ERP for schedules, collaboration, and construction project tracking.



- **[Frappe Framework](https://github.com/frappe/frappe)**  

  Open-source low-code framework underlying ERPNext and BuildSuite—used to build custom construction workflows and doctypes.



- **[Community job-cost and WIP open models](https://github.com/)**  

  Scripts and templates for job cost ledgers, work-in-progress calculations, and basic construction accounting patterns.



- **[Document and drawing open control tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Complementary open document management used with construction ERP for RFIs, submittals, and plan sets.



- **[Payroll and certified payroll open experiments](https://github.com/)**  

  Limited open approaches to labor costing; most certified payroll remains commercial.



- **[Documentation and ERPNext construction playbooks](https://docs.erpnext.com/)**  

  Guides for configuring projects, materials, and costing for contractor-style organizations.



- **[Self-hosted construction stack patterns](https://buildsuite.io/)**  

  Patterns combining ERPNext/BuildSuite with open PM and document tools for smaller contractors.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **ERPNext** or **BuildSuite** when full data ownership and no per-seat fees are priorities.

- Extending **Odoo Community** with project, inventory, and accounting modules for lighter contractor needs.

- Using **OpenProject** for schedule and collaboration alongside open or commercial financials.

- Accepting that deep AIA progress billing, certified payroll, union rules, equipment costing, multi-company construction accounting, and mature field integrations still favor commercial construction ERPs (Viewpoint, CMiC, Acumatica Construction, Foundation, ComputerEase, Sage, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on cost control, customization, and avoiding vendor lock-in for mid-size firms with technical capacity.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Run ERPNext or BuildSuite for core financials and projects → add OpenProject for schedules → manage documents in open DMS → report job cost in open BI. Suitable for smaller contractors and organizations with in-house or partner technical support. Larger GCs typically run commercial construction ERP.
